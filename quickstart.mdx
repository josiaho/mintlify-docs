---
title: "Get Started with Tunnelvision in Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Download Tunnelvision, block distracting apps, add a habit, and hand the unlock button to a trusted friend — all in under five minutes."
---

Tunnelvision turns your phone's most distracting apps into something you have to earn back. Instead of relying on willpower alone, you set a daily screen time limit, build habits as proof of focus, and give a trusted friend the power to approve — or deny — your unlock requests. This guide walks you through every step from download to your first accountability loop.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Tunnelvision">
    Tunnelvision requires **iOS 18.4 or later**. Open the App Store on your iPhone and tap the link below to install the app.

    [Download on the App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tunnelvision-habit-tracker/id6752546483)

    Once the app finishes installing, open it to begin setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    On the welcome screen, tap **Get Started** and sign up with your Apple ID or email address. Your account is what lets your accountability partner find you and respond to unlock requests, so use a name your friend will recognise.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose apps to block and set a daily limit">
    Tunnelvision will prompt you to grant Screen Time permission — this is required for hard app blocks enforced at the OS level.

    1. Tap **Add Apps** and select the apps you want to block (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Snapchat, or any others).
    2. Set your **daily time limit** — for example, 30 minutes per day across all selected apps.
    3. Tap **Save**. Once you hit that limit, Tunnelvision locks those apps until your friend approves an unlock.

    You can also enable **website blocking** to cover browser-based versions of the same distractions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add at least one habit">
    Habits are the proof you show your friend before requesting an unlock. Tap **Add Habit** and create something meaningful — a morning workout, a 20-minute reading session, or a study block.

    - Free accounts support up to **3 habits**.
    - Upgrade to Premium for **unlimited habits** and full focus schedules.

    The more habits you complete before sending an unlock request, the stronger your case looks to your friend.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your accountability partner">
    Tap **Friends** in the navigation bar, then **Invite a Friend**. Enter their name or share the invite link directly. Your friend downloads Tunnelvision and accepts your request — they become your **gatekeeper**.

    One trusted friend is all you need. Choose someone who will actually push back when you haven't earned it yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hit your screen time limit">
    Use your blocked apps as normal. When you reach your daily limit, Tunnelvision kicks in and the apps become inaccessible. You'll see a lock screen letting you know it's time to focus on something real instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your habits, then send an unlock request">
    Work through your habits — log that workout, finish your reading, wrap up your study session. As you mark each habit complete, Tunnelvision records your progress.

    When you're ready, tap **Request Unlock** and choose how much extra screen time you'd like. Your request — including your completed habits for the day — is sent straight to your friend.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your friend approves and you get extra screen time">
    Your friend receives a notification showing exactly what you completed and your recent streak. If they're satisfied, they tap **Approve** and your blocked apps unlock for the requested duration. When that time is up, the block kicks back in automatically.

    If your friend denies the request or doesn't respond, you stay locked out — that's the whole point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Pick the right accountability partner.** The best choice is someone who genuinely wants you to succeed but isn't afraid to say no. A close friend, sibling, or partner who understands your goals works far better than someone who will approve every request without a second thought. Talk to them before you send the invite — let them know you actually want them to hold the line.
</Tip>
