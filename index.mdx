Tunnelvision puts you back in control of your phone by turning screen time into something you have to earn. You set hard blocks on the apps that pull you off track — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and more — then hand the unlock button to a trusted friend. When your daily limit runs out, the only way back in is to complete your habits and send your friend a request they can approve or deny. No workarounds, no sneaky bypasses — just accountability that actually works.

## Why Tunnelvision?

Most screen time apps rely on willpower — you set a limit, get a gentle nudge when you hit it, and tap "ignore" without a second thought. Tunnelvision removes that escape hatch entirely. Blocks are hard, unlocks require effort, and a real person who cares about you is watching. Here is what makes that possible:
