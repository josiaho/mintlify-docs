Tunnelvision tracks your habit completions day by day and builds a streak — a running count of consecutive days where you showed up and did the work. Your streak isn't just a number for your own motivation; it's also visible to your accountability partner every time you send an unlock request. This guide explains how streaks work, how to view your progress, and how to keep the momentum going.

## What a Streak Is

A streak is the number of consecutive days on which you completed your habits. Every day you log all your habits, the count goes up by one. The streak reflects consistent effort over time — not a single good day.

## Why Streaks Matter

Your accountability partner sees your current streak length when reviewing your unlock requests. A long streak is a signal that you're genuinely engaging with your habits and not just logging them to unlock your phone. It gives your partner meaningful context before they approve or deny your request.

<Note>
  Streak data is shared with your accountability partner as part of every unlock request. They can see both your current streak length and your recent habit completion history before making a decision.
</Note>

## How Streaks Reset

Missing a day — meaning no habit completions are logged before midnight — breaks your streak and resets the count to zero. The streak requires consecutive daily completions; a gap of even one day starts you over.

<Tip>
  Don't let perfect be the enemy of good. A broken streak is a reason to restart, not quit. Every long streak started at day one — just log your habits today and begin again.
</Tip>

## How to View Your Streak

Your current streak is displayed on your **home screen** and in your **profile**. You can check your progress at any time without navigating away from the main view.

## What Your Friend Sees

When you send an unlock request, your accountability partner sees:

- **Your current streak length** — the number of consecutive days you've completed your habits
- **Recent habit completion history** — a look at which habits you've been logging and how consistently

This information is shown to them before they make any decision, so maintaining a genuine streak has a real impact on how your requests are received.
