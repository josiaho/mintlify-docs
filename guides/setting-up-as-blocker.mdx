---
title: "Set Up Tunnelvision as the Person Getting Blocked"
sidebarTitle: "Blocker Setup"
description: "Configure Tunnelvision on your iPhone to block distracting apps, set daily time limits, and hand the unlock button to a trusted friend."
---

Tunnelvision puts you back in control of your screen time by letting a trusted friend hold the unlock button. This guide walks you through everything you need to do on your own phone — from granting permissions to sending your first unlock request.

<Warning>
  Revoking Screen Time permissions will break the hard blocks. If Tunnelvision loses access to Screen Time, your selected apps will no longer be blocked even after you hit your daily limit. Keep Screen Time permissions enabled for the app to function correctly.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and open Tunnelvision">
    Install Tunnelvision from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tunnelvision-habit-tracker/id6752546483) on your iPhone. Open the app and follow the initial onboarding prompts to create your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant Screen Time permissions">
    When prompted, allow Tunnelvision to use Screen Time. This permission is required for hard blocks — without it, the app cannot enforce limits on your selected apps. Follow the on-screen instructions to authorize access in your iPhone's Settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the apps you want to block">
    Choose the apps you want Tunnelvision to restrict. You can block individual apps like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, and Snapchat, or select entire categories such as Social Networking or Entertainment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your daily time limit">
    For each app or category you selected, set a daily time limit. Once you reach that limit, Tunnelvision locks you out until an unlock is approved by your accountability partner.

    <Tip>
      Start with a realistic daily limit rather than the most aggressive one. A limit you can actually stick to — even if it's 30 minutes — is far more effective than one you'll be tempted to work around immediately.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your habits">
    Habits are the tasks you must complete and log before you can request extra screen time. Tap **Add Habit** and set up the activities that represent real effort — workouts, reading sessions, study blocks, or anything else meaningful to you. Free accounts support up to 3 habits; upgrade to Premium for unlimited habits.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your accountability partner">
    Go to the **Friends** tab and send an invitation to the person you trust to hold your unlock button. They'll need to have Tunnelvision installed on their own iPhone to accept.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for them to accept">
    Your accountability partner will receive your invitation and can accept it from their own device. Once they accept, the connection is active and your blocks are fully enforced. You're protected.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Request an unlock when you need one">
    When you hit your daily limit, complete your habits and log them inside Tunnelvision. Then tap **Request Unlock** to send a notification to your accountability partner. They'll review your completed habits before deciding whether to approve or deny your request.
  </Step>
</Steps>
