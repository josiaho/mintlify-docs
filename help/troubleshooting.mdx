---
title: "Fix Common Issues With Tunnelvision on iPhone & iPad"
sidebarTitle: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Diagnose and fix common Tunnelvision problems including app blocks not working, missing unlock notifications, and Premium purchase restoration."
---

Most Tunnelvision issues come down to permissions, an outdated app version, or a device that doesn't meet the minimum requirements. Work through the relevant section below to resolve your issue quickly.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The app block isn't working">
    Tunnelvision relies on Apple's Screen Time framework to enforce blocks. If blocked apps are still opening, follow these steps:

    1. Open **iPhone Settings** and tap **Screen Time**.
    2. Make sure Screen Time is turned on.
    3. Confirm that Tunnelvision has the required Screen Time permissions.
    4. Restart the app after granting permissions.

    Tunnelvision requires **iOS 18.4 or later**. If your device is running an older version of iOS, update it in **Settings → General → Software Update**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My friend isn't receiving unlock request notifications">
    If your accountability friend isn't seeing your unlock requests, their notification permissions may be turned off. Ask them to take these steps on their device:

    1. Open **iPhone Settings** and tap **Notifications**.
    2. Scroll down and tap **Tunnelvision**.
    3. Toggle **Allow Notifications** on.
    4. Make sure **Alerts** and **Lock Screen** delivery are enabled.

    After adjusting the settings, send a test unlock request to confirm notifications are coming through.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find the app limits window">
    This issue was resolved in **version 1.1.9**. Update Tunnelvision to the latest version via the App Store and the app limits window will appear correctly.

    To update: open the **App Store**, tap your profile icon in the top-right corner, scroll to Tunnelvision, and tap **Update**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Website blocking isn't working">
    Website blocking was also fixed end-to-end in **version 1.1.9**. First, update the app via the App Store using the steps above.

    If websites are still accessible after updating, check your configuration:

    1. Open Tunnelvision and go to the **Limits** section.
    2. Confirm the websites you want to block are listed there — not just under the app blocking section.
    3. Save your changes and test again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I lost my streak after reinstalling the app">
    Reinstalling Tunnelvision may clear locally stored data, including your habit history and streak. To attempt recovery, contact the support team at **info.tunnelvisionapp@gmail.com** and include your Apple ID email and an approximate date range for your streak. The team will do their best to help restore your history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't restore my Premium purchase">
    If Premium features aren't available after a reinstall or on a new device, restore your purchase:

    1. Open Tunnelvision and go to **Settings**.
    2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
    3. Confirm you are signed in with the **same Apple ID** used for the original purchase.

    If restoration still fails, the issue may be on Apple's side. Contact **Apple Support** directly for billing and purchase help. You can also reach the Tunnelvision team at **info.tunnelvisionapp@gmail.com** if you need further assistance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app doesn't work on my device">
    Tunnelvision has the following minimum requirements:

    - **iPhone:** iOS 18.4 or later
    - **iPad:** iPadOS 18.4 or later
    - **Mac:** macOS 15.4 or later with an Apple M1 chip or newer

    To check your iOS or iPadOS version, go to **Settings → General → About**. If your device supports the required OS version, update via **Settings → General → Software Update**. Older devices that cannot run iOS 18.4 are not compatible with Tunnelvision.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Card title="Still need help?" icon="envelope" href="mailto:info.tunnelvisionapp@gmail.com">
  If none of the steps above resolve your issue, reach out to the Tunnelvision support team directly at **info.tunnelvisionapp@gmail.com**. Include a description of the problem, your device model, and your iOS version so the team can assist you as quickly as possible.
</Card>
