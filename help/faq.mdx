Tunnelvision combines Apple's Screen Time framework with real human accountability to help you reclaim your focus. If you have a question about how the app works, your subscription, or what your friend can see, you'll find the answers below.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does my friend need the app too?">
    Yes. Your accountability friend must download Tunnelvision to receive and act on your unlock requests. Once they have the app installed and an account set up, you can add them as your accountability partner from within the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I bypass the block myself?">
    Hard blocks are enforced by Apple's Screen Time framework at the OS level, so you cannot bypass them on your own without going through the unlock request flow. The only other way to remove a block is to disable Screen Time entirely in your iPhone Settings — but doing so breaks the protection and defeats the purpose of having an accountability partner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my friend doesn't respond?">
    You stay locked out until your accountability friend approves the request or your current schedule ends. For best results, choose a friend who is responsive and available during the hours you typically need support. Let them know in advance that you may reach out for accountability.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have more than one accountability friend?">
    The Free plan supports a limited number of accountability friends. Upgrading to Premium unlocks support for more friends, so you can build a wider accountability network and always have someone available to respond.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the block work on Wi-Fi and cellular?">
    Yes. Hard blocks work regardless of your network connection — whether you're on Wi-Fi, cellular, or even offline. Because blocking is enforced at the OS level by Apple's Screen Time API, your network status has no effect on whether apps or websites remain blocked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a Focus Schedule?">
    A Focus Schedule is a recurring, time-based block you set up in advance — for example, blocking social media apps from 9 am to 12 pm on weekdays. Once active, the schedule automatically engages and disengages the block at the times you choose without any manual action on your part. Focus Schedules are a Premium feature.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my streak?">
    You don't need to reset your streak manually. Streaks reset automatically if you go an entire day without completing any habits. Stay consistent each day to keep your streak growing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is website blocking included?">
    Yes. In addition to blocking apps like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, and Snapchat, you can block specific websites. Configure which sites to block in the Limits section of the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Tunnelvision on iPad and Mac?">
    Yes. Tunnelvision supports iPhone (iOS 18.4+), iPad (iPadOS 18.4+), and Mac (macOS 15.4+ with an Apple M1 chip or later). Make sure your device meets the minimum OS requirement before installing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does billing work?">
    Tunnelvision Premium is billed through Apple's App Store. You can choose a monthly plan at $8.99/month or an annual plan at $69.99/year, both of which include a 7-day free trial. To cancel at any time, go to **iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions**, select Tunnelvision, and tap **Cancel Subscription**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Tunnelvision work on Android?">
    No. Tunnelvision is built on Apple's Screen Time framework, which is exclusive to Apple platforms. The app is currently available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac only, with no Android version planned at this time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What data does Tunnelvision share with my friend?">
    Tunnelvision shares only the information your friend needs to hold you accountable: your habit completions, your current streak length, and the details of any unlock request you send. Your actual app usage statistics and browsing history are never shared with anyone — all usage data stays on your device.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
