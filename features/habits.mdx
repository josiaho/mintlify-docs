Habits are the daily tasks you commit to before you can ask for more screen time. When you send an unlock request to your friend, they see exactly which habits you've already completed that day — giving them real evidence to weigh before they approve or deny. The more you've done, the stronger your case.

## What Habits Are

A habit is any daily task you want to hold yourself to. Tunnelvision doesn't prescribe what your habits should be — you define them based on what matters to you. Common examples include:

- **Workout** — 30 minutes of exercise
- **Reading** — one chapter or 20 minutes of a book
- **Study** — a focused work or study session
- **Meditation** — a morning or evening mindfulness practice
- **Journaling** — writing at least a few sentences

Each habit is binary: either you did it today or you didn't. There's no partial credit — just done or not done.

## How Habits Become Proof

When you request extra screen time, your friend receives a notification that shows:

- Every habit you've set for yourself
- Which ones you've marked complete today
- How many minutes you're requesting

If you've completed all your habits before asking, your friend sees a full checklist. If you haven't done any, they see an empty one. The difference is visible, and it shifts the social dynamic: you're not just asking for a favor — you're presenting evidence that you've already earned it.

## How to Add a Habit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Habits tab">
    Tap **Habits** in the bottom navigation bar inside Tunnelvision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Habit">
    Tap the **+** button to create a new habit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your habit">
    Give it a clear, specific name — something you'll recognize at a glance. "Workout" is better than "Exercise goals." Specific names make it harder to rationalize skipping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Tap **Save**. Your habit is now part of every unlock request you send today and going forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Marking a Habit Complete

When you finish a habit, open Tunnelvision and check it off on the Habits tab. Marked habits turn green and stay visible on your home screen so you know what you've accomplished before you reach your screen time limit.

You can mark habits complete at any point during the day — before or after your limit runs out. Completing a habit while you're still within your limit still counts toward your unlock request later.

## How More Completed Habits Strengthen Your Request

Your friend can see the difference between a request that comes with zero habits completed and one that comes with all of them checked off. There's no algorithmic scoring — it's a human judgment call — but the social pressure is real.

Completing more habits before you request extra time:

- Signals that you've done what you set out to do
- Makes it easier for your friend to say yes in good conscience
- Builds a streak history over time that your friend can review across requests

If you ask for more time without completing anything, your friend has very little reason to approve. That accountability is the core of how Tunnelvision works.

<Tip>
  Keep your habits specific and binary. "Read for 20 minutes" is better than "Read more." "Complete one workout" is better than "Be active." Binary habits are easier to mark honestly — you either did the thing or you didn't — which makes them more credible proof when your friend reviews your request.
</Tip>
