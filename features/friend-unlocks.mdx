The core idea behind Tunnelvision is simple: you shouldn't be in charge of your own unlock button. Willpower runs out, and self-imposed timers are easy to dismiss when you're the one holding the override. Tunnelvision hands that control to someone who knows you and cares about your goals — a friend who sees your proof of work before deciding whether to let you back in.

## How the Gatekeeper System Works

When your daily screen time limit runs out, you can request extra minutes from your designated friend. Your friend receives a notification, reviews your completed habits for the day, and makes the call. If they approve, you get the time you asked for — then the block reactivates automatically when those minutes run out. If they deny, you stay locked out.

You cannot bypass this. The unlock is not stored on your device. Only your friend can issue it.

## Inviting a Friend

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Friends tab">
    Tap **Friends** in the Tunnelvision navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Invite a Friend">
    Tap **+ Invite** and choose how to send the invite — via Messages, AirDrop, or a shareable link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your friend accepts and installs Tunnelvision">
    Your friend needs the Tunnelvision app on their own iOS device to receive and act on unlock requests. They'll be prompted to install it when they open the invite link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Once your friend accepts, they appear in your Friends list as an active gatekeeper. You can now send them unlock requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Your Friend Receives

When you send an unlock request, your friend gets a push notification on their device. Tapping it opens a request summary that shows:

- **Your habit checklist** — every habit you've set, and which ones you've marked complete today
- **Minutes requested** — how much extra time you're asking for
- **Your streak history** — your completion streak over recent days, so they can see whether you've been consistent or this is a pattern of repeated requests

Your friend sees the full picture before making any decision.

## What Your Friend Can Do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Approve" icon="circle-check">
    Your friend taps **Approve** and you receive the requested minutes immediately. Once those minutes run out, the block reactivates automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deny" icon="circle-xmark">
    Your friend taps **Deny** and you stay locked out. No extra time is granted. You can complete more habits and try again later.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Choosing the Right Friend

The gatekeeper system is only as strong as the person you choose. The best gatekeeper is someone who:

- Knows you well enough to tell when you're slacking
- Genuinely cares about the goals you've told them about
- Won't automatically approve every request just to avoid awkwardness

A friend who always approves is no gatekeeper at all. Pick someone who will hold you to your own standards — even when you push back.

<Note>
  Be upfront with your friend about what you're asking of them. Show them your habit list, explain your goals, and let them know you want honest decisions — not automatic approvals. The accountability only works if your gatekeeper understands the role.
</Note>
