Focus schedules let you define time windows when certain apps are always blocked — no daily setup required. Set a schedule once and Tunnelvision enforces it every time that window comes around. Whether you need silence during work hours, distraction-free study sessions, or a hard stop before bed, focus schedules remove the decision entirely.

## What Focus Schedules Are

A focus schedule is a recurring block rule tied to specific days and times. You choose which apps or categories to block, pick the days of the week it applies, and set the start and end time. When that window opens, the block activates automatically. When the window closes, apps become available again — until the next scheduled window.

Unlike daily time limits, focus schedules are not based on how long you've used an app. They're purely time-of-day rules. If a scheduled block is active, the apps are blocked — regardless of whether you've used them at all that day.

## Common Use Cases

- **Work hours** — Block social media from 9 AM to 5 PM on weekdays so you stay focused at your desk
- **Study sessions** — Lock out distractions from 7 PM to 10 PM on weeknights during exam season
- **Bedtime** — Block everything from 10 PM to 7 AM so your phone doesn't keep you up

## How to Create a Focus Schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Schedules tab">
    Tap **Schedules** in the Tunnelvision navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap New Schedule">
    Tap **+ New Schedule** to open the schedule builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the apps or categories to block">
    Select individual apps, entire categories (Social Networking, Entertainment, Games), or specific websites. You can combine them in a single schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the days of the week">
    Toggle on the days this schedule should apply. You can create different schedules for weekdays vs. weekends if your routine differs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time window">
    Pick a start time and an end time. The block activates at the start time and lifts at the end time automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and save your schedule">
    Give your schedule a recognizable name — "Deep Work," "Study Block," "Sleep Mode" — and tap **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Schedules Activate

Once a schedule is saved, you don't have to do anything. Tunnelvision monitors the time and activates the block the moment the window opens. Apps go from available to blocked automatically — you won't receive a warning beforehand.

When the window ends, apps become available again without any action on your part. If you want to end a schedule early, you'll need your friend's approval — the same way you'd request extra time under a daily limit.

<Tip>
  Combine focus schedules with daily habit goals for maximum accountability. Use a schedule to enforce your deep work block during the day, then set habits — like completing your workout or finishing a study session — as the proof you need to unlock apps afterward. The schedule removes temptation during work hours; the habits give you something to earn back in the evening.
</Tip>
