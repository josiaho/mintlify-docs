Tunnelvision offers two plans so you can start building better habits right away, then upgrade when you're ready for more. The free plan gives you everything you need to get started: app blocking, an accountability friend, and up to three habits. Premium unlocks unlimited habits, full focus schedules, and every feature added in the future.

## What's Included

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Free Plan — Forever Free">
    - **3 habits** to earn your screen time back
    - App blocking for distracting apps (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Snapchat)
    - Accountability friend to hold your unlock button
    - Daily screen time limits
    - Unlock requests sent to your friend after completing habits
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Premium Plan">
    - **Unlimited habits** — build as many routines as you need
    - **Full focus schedules** — set recurring time blocks to automatically enforce limits throughout your day
    - All future features as they are released
    - Everything in the free plan, plus more
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Pricing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Monthly — $8.99/month">
    Billed monthly. Cancel anytime through your Apple ID settings. Includes a **7-day free trial** for new subscribers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Yearly — $69.99/year (Best Value)">
    Billed once per year. Saves roughly 35% compared to the monthly plan. Includes a **7-day free trial** for new subscribers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Yearly Intro Offer — $34.99 for your first year">
    Available to new Premium subscribers only. Your first year is billed at **$34.99**, then renews at the standard $69.99/year rate. Includes a **7-day free trial**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
