Tunnelvision Premium is billed through Apple's in-app purchase system, so your subscription lives alongside every other Apple subscription you manage. You can start a free trial, switch plans, cancel, or restore a purchase entirely from your device — no separate account or password required.

## Start a Free Trial

New Premium subscribers get a **7-day free trial** before any charge. To activate it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tunnelvision">
    Launch the app on your iPhone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Premium banner">
    You'll find it on the main screen or inside Settings. Tap it to open the upgrade screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select **Monthly ($8.99/month)**, **Yearly ($69.99/year)**, or the **Intro Yearly offer ($34.99 for your first year)** if it's available to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in the App Store">
    Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password. Your free trial starts immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upgrade from the Free Plan

Upgrading works exactly the same as starting a free trial. Open Tunnelvision, tap the Premium banner, choose your plan, and confirm in the App Store. Your new features unlock right away.

<Tip>
  The yearly plan at $69.99 saves roughly 35% compared to paying $8.99 every month. If you're committed to the habit, the yearly plan is the better deal.
</Tip>

## Cancel Your Subscription

Tunnelvision subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. To cancel:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open iPhone Settings">
    Tap the **Settings** app on your home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap your Apple ID">
    Your name appears at the top of Settings. Tap it to open your Apple ID page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Subscriptions">
    Find and tap **Subscriptions** to see all your active and expired subscriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Tunnelvision">
    Tap **Tunnelvision** in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Cancel Subscription">
    Confirm the cancellation. You keep Premium access until the end of your current billing period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Restore a Purchase

If you reinstall Tunnelvision or switch to a new iPhone, your Premium status may not appear automatically. To restore it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tunnelvision">
    Launch the app on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Tap the settings icon inside the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Restore Purchases">
    Tunnelvision will check your Apple ID for an active subscription and restore your access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Family Sharing

Some Tunnelvision in-app purchases may be eligible for Apple Family Sharing, allowing family members in your Family Sharing group to access Premium features. Availability depends on Apple's Family Sharing rules and the specific purchase type. Check your Family Sharing settings in the iPhone Settings app to see what's shared.

## Billing Issues and Refunds

<Note>
  Tunnelvision uses Apple's in-app purchase system. Tunnelvision cannot process refunds directly — contact [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/billing) for any billing issues or refund requests.
</Note>
