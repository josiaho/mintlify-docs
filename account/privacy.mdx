Privacy is built into how Tunnelvision works, not bolted on afterward. Because the app runs on Apple's Screen Time framework, your app usage stays entirely on your device — Tunnelvision never sees which apps you open, how long you spend in them, or anything you do inside them. What gets shared with your accountability partner is only what you choose to send.

## Your Data Stays on Your Device

Tunnelvision does not collect or transmit your app usage data. The Screen Time limits, block enforcement, and usage tracking all happen locally through Apple's Screen Time API. Tunnelvision cannot see which apps you opened, how many times you tried to unlock a blocked app, or what you did while using any app.

## What Your Accountability Partner Sees

Your accountability friend only receives information that you actively send to them. That includes:

- **Your completed habits for the day** — so they can see you've done the work before approving an unlock
- **Your current streak** — to give them context on your progress
- **Your unlock request details** — the request you send when you want more screen time

Your friend does not see your raw screen time data, your app usage history, or anything beyond what you explicitly share when making a request.

## What Tunnelvision Does Not Collect

Tunnelvision does not collect, store, or transmit:

- Your app usage details or screen time history
- Your browsing history or in-app activity
- Your screen content or anything displayed on your screen
- Any behavioral data used for advertising

## No Ads, No Data Sales

Tunnelvision does not show ads. It does not sell your data to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party. The only revenue model is the Premium subscription — your attention is not the product.

## Apple's Screen Time Framework

Tunnelvision enforces app blocks using Apple's Screen Time API. Apple's own privacy model governs how that framework operates. Tunnelvision requests only the permissions it needs to set limits and respond to unlock requests — it does not have broad access to your device beyond those functions.

## App Store Privacy Label

Per Tunnelvision's App Store disclosure, the following data categories may be linked to your identity:

- **Contact Info** — used to connect you with your accountability partner
- **User Content** — the habits and unlock requests you create
- **Identifiers** — to associate your account with your device
